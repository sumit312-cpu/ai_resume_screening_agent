- SQL
- Machine Learning

- NumPy

Preferred Skills:

- Deep Learning
- TensorFlow / PyTorch
- Power BI or Tableau

Experience:
- 1–3 years of experience in Data Science or related field

Education:
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field
